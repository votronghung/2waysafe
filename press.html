2WaySafe — Press kit

Following distance from the iPhone camera, on-device.

In one sentence

2WaySafe turns an iPhone on the windshield into a following-distance companion: it recognizes the vehicle in your lane and shows your gap as one large number — seconds, or meters where the law states the gap in meters.

Key features

  • Country-aware: Vietnam's legal minimum-distance table (Circular 38/2024), “half the speedometer” in Germany, Austria and Switzerland, a 2- or 3-second rule elsewhere; feet and mph where the iPhone uses them.
  • Lane-aware: follows the vehicle in your own lane using the painted lane markings, steadily.
  • Weather-aware: recognizes wiper sweeps and snow-covered roads by itself and widens the reminder threshold; when the view is blocked it says so instead of guessing.
  • Made for the car: one big number and one button, optional sound reminder, the screen dims itself to limit heat, measuring resumes after a call, Live Activity on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.
  • Tested on real roads: developed with weekly highway drives against a car-mounted reference radar.

Important

Readings are AI estimates for information only and can be wrong or late (night, glare, heavy rain, dirty windshield, partly hidden vehicles). 2WaySafe is not a safety or driver-assistance system and does not replace the driver's attention. Please do not film or operate a phone while driving.
